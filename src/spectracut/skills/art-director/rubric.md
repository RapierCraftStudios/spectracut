# Art director rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## palette (weight 1.5)

Is the palette coherent with the brand and the Look, with true product colours?

## materials (weight 1.0)

Do materials read clearly on camera at delivery size?

## typography (weight 1.5)

Does typography follow the brand system and stay legible in every format?

## brand-safety (weight 2.0)

Is every frame free of unlicensed third-party brands, logos and marks?

## look (weight 1.0)

Is the Look declared, consistent across shots and within its QA thresholds?
