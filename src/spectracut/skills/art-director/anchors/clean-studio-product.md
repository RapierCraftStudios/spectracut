# Taste anchor: Clean studio product look

A product on a seamless backdrop with a two-tone gradient, soft top light and crisp rim highlights. Materials are honest: anodised metal shows its grain only in the macro shot. There is nothing in frame that isn't the product or a deliberate, unbranded support.

## What to take from it

- Restraint: one hero, one accent colour.
- Edges defined by light, not by outlines or glow.
