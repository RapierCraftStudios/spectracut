# Motion designer rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## purpose (weight 1.5)

Does every motion explain something the VO or beat says?

## ui-life (weight 2.0)

Is the UI alive with visible actions and state changes, never a held screenshot?

## easing (weight 1.0)

Is the motion eased with weight and timing that feel deliberate?

## legibility (weight 1.5)

Is animated text legible and held for its reading time?
