# Sound designer rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## sync (weight 1.5)

Are effects tightly in sync with the actions they belong to?

## restraint (weight 1.5)

Is sound design supportive and restrained, leaving space for VO and music?

## consistency (weight 1.0)

Is the sound palette consistent across the film?

## licensing (weight 1.5)

Is every sound original or commercially licensed with provenance?
