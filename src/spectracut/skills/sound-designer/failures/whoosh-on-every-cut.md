# Failure: A whoosh on every cut

**Seen in:** AlterLab launch trailer, first sound pass

## What went wrong

Every cut got a transition whoosh. They masked VO consonants and made the film sound like a template.

## How to detect it

- Transition effects on more than about a quarter of cuts.
- Transitions overlapping VO words.

## The fix

Keep transitions for the few structural moments (opening, reveal, end card). Let most cuts be carried by the music.
