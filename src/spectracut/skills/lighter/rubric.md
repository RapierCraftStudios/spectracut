# Lighter rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## exposure (weight 2.0)

Is the subject exposed correctly and every screen exposed for its UI?

## clarity (weight 2.0)

Is the frame free of haze, crushed blacks and veiling over the product or screens?

## shape (weight 1.5)

Does the lighting shape the product with clear edges and controlled reflections?

## consistency (weight 1.0)

Is lighting consistent across shots of the same scene and within the Look?
