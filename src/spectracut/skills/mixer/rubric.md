# Mixer rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## intelligibility (weight 2.0)

Is the VO clearly intelligible over music and effects throughout?

## loudness (weight 1.5)

Does the master meet the delivery spec's loudness and true-peak targets?

## balance (weight 1.5)

Are music, effects and VO balanced with dynamics that support the story?

## deliverables (weight 1.0)

Are stems and the M&E track complete and consistent with the master?
