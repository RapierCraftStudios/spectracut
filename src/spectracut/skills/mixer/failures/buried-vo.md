# Failure: Buried VO

**Seen in:** AlterLab launch trailer, temp mix

## What went wrong

The music was mixed to sound big on studio monitors; on laptop speakers the VO disappeared under the drop.

## How to detect it

- VO-to-music level difference under about 6 dB during speech.
- Words unintelligible on a small-speaker check.

## The fix

Sidechain-duck the music under the VO, carve speech frequencies, and check the mix on small speakers before the master.
