# Failure: Real-brand leaks

**Seen in:** AlterLab launch trailer, set dressing and device models

## What went wrong

A downloaded laptop model kept its manufacturer's logo on the lid, a stock desk prop carried a real coffee-chain cup, and a browser capture showed a third-party site's logo in a tab. None were licensed by the brief owner.

## How to detect it

- Logos, wordmarks or distinctive trade dress on props, devices, clothing or captured screens.
- Asset provenance records naming a manufacturer model or a brand.
- Browser chrome, tabs, bookmarks or notifications visible in captures.
- Text in frame that is not in the brief.

## The fix

Use generic or own-brand devices with logos removed, dress sets with unbranded props, and crop or clean captures to the product's own UI. Anything branded must be supplied and licensed by the brief owner and recorded in its provenance.
