# Critic rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## accuracy (weight 2.0)

Do the scores reflect what is visible in the frames, consistent with human ratings?

## coverage (weight 1.5)

Does the review check every rubric criterion, the brief, the canon and the failure corpus?

## actionability (weight 1.5)

Are notes specific enough for the department to fix the problem?

## bar (weight 1.0)

Is the review judged against the taste anchors, distinguishing good from great?
