# Taste anchor: One Beam

The target frame is a stack of five glass slabs, lit so each edge is drawn by a thin rim highlight against a dark gradient. A single emissive beam passes through the stack and is the brightest element in the frame. An engraved readout in a metal plate below the stack is lit by a raking light and reads clearly at delivery size.

## What to take from it

- One subject, centred and filling the frame; the background stays out of the way.
- Contrast comes from light placement (key, fill, rim) and one emissive accent, never from fog.
- Physical materials: real glass with refraction and edge highlights, brushed metal for the plate.
- Text is engraved, lit and legible, and it is the brief's copy only.
