# Scene builder rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## adherence (weight 2.0)

Is every object, material, light and camera the description names present and recognisable?

## composition (weight 1.5)

Is there one clear subject, deliberately framed, with nothing important cut by the edge?

## lighting (weight 1.5)

Does key, fill and rim light shape the subject, with no haze over it and no flat or blown areas?

## materials (weight 1.0)

Do surfaces read as physical materials (glass, metal, plastic) with macro detail the light reveals?

## legibility (weight 1.0)

Is all on-screen text readable at delivery size, and only the brief's copy?

## palette (weight 1.0)

Do the colours stay in the art direction's palette and the Look's thresholds?
