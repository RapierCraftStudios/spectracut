# Storyboard rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## clarity (weight 1.5)

Does each board read at a glance: subject, size, action and move?

## continuity (weight 1.0)

Is screen direction and continuity consistent across the boards?

## safe-areas (weight 1.0)

Are text and key action inside the safe areas for every delivery aspect?

## timing (weight 1.0)

Does the animatic timing match the VO and the shot plan?
