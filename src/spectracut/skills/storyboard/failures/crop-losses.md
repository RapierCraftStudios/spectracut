# Failure: Key action lost in vertical crops

**Seen in:** AlterLab launch trailer, social cut-downs

## What went wrong

Boards were framed only for 16:9. In the 9:16 cut-down the cursor and the result panel fell outside the crop, so the vertical edit showed an empty half of the screen.

## How to detect it

- Key action or on-screen text outside the 9:16 or 1:1 safe crop on a board.
- Boards with no crop marks.

## The fix

Mark every delivery crop on the board and keep the key action in the shared centre region, or plan a separate reframe for the vertical edit.
