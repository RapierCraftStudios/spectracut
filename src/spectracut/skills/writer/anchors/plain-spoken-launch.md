# Taste anchor: A plain-spoken developer launch

The target voice for developer products: a calm narrator who states the problem in one line, shows the product solving it, and gets out of the way. No hype words, no rhetorical questions stacked three deep, and every claim shown on screen as it is said.

## What to take from it

- One idea per line, said once.
- Proof is shown, not asserted.
- The call to action is specific (what to do, where).
