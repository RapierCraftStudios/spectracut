# Failure: Invented claims

**Seen in:** AlterLab launch trailer, first script draft

## What went wrong

The draft promised '10x faster' and named customer counts that were not in the brief. They sounded good and had no source.

## How to detect it

- A number, comparison or customer reference with no brief source id.
- Superlatives (fastest, best, only) without a cited proof point.

## The fix

Map each line to a brief source in the beat sheet. Cut or rewrite any line without one, and ask the Producer for a source rather than guessing.
