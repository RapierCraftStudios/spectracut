# Failure: Repeated push-ins

**Seen in:** AlterLab launch trailer, first assembly

## What went wrong

Almost every shot used the same slow dolly push-in toward the subject. Individually each looked fine; cut together the film had one rhythm, no variety of size or direction, and every beat felt equally (un)important.

## How to detect it

- Three or more consecutive shots with the same move type and direction.
- More than about a third of a sequence's shots are push-ins.
- Shot list camera notes repeat the same move.

## The fix

Give each move a reason: push in only for a reveal or emphasis. Vary shot size, angle and move (static, lateral track, orbit, pull-out, rack focus) across a sequence, and cut on motion so moves alternate direction.
