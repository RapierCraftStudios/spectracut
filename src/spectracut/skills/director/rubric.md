# Director rubric

The Critic scores each criterion in [0, 1]; the score is the weight-averaged criterion score. The same criteria are in `rubric.json`, which the benchmark runner scores.

## intent (weight 2.0)

Does every shot serve a beat of the script and the brief's core message?

## camera-variety (weight 1.5)

Do shot sizes, angles and moves vary with clear motivation, without repeated push-ins?

## staging (weight 1.5)

Is the subject clearly staged, with screens readable and composition deliberate?

## pacing (weight 1.0)

Does the shot plan's timing fit the VO and the target length?

## style-translation (weight 1.0)

Are style references translated into concrete, original attributes?
