# Taste anchor: Motivated camera in a product film

Top product films move the camera only when it tells you something: a lateral track reveals the profile, an orbit shows the thickness, a cut to a macro insert lands the detail the narrator names. Between moves the frame holds still and lets the product speak.

## What to take from it

- Every move answers 'what does this show that a still frame can't?'.
- Rhythm comes from the cut, not from constant motion.
