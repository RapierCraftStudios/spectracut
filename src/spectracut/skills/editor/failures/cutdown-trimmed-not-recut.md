# Failure: Cut-down trimmed, not re-cut

**Seen in:** AlterLab launch film, first 15 s and 30 s deliveries

## What went wrong

The 15 s and 30 s cut-downs kept the master's shots and shrank each one in proportion to fit the length. Cuts drifted off the music's beats, interludes ran as long (relatively) as in the master, and the 15 s felt like a rushed 60 s instead of a film of its own.

## How to detect it

- Cut-down shot lengths keep the master's ratios (a proportional trim).
- Cuts that do not land on a beat of the cut-down's music.
- The 15 s cut-down's shots are not quicker than the 30 s cut-down's.

## The fix

Re-pace each cut-down on the beat: hook first, device shots in whole beats (shorter at 15 s than at 30 s), interludes as 1–2-beat punctuation, end card last taking the remainder so the cut is frame-exact.
