# Failure: On-screen text cut too fast

**Seen in:** AlterLab launch trailer, first assembly

## What went wrong

Shots with feature callouts were cut to the music at under a second. Viewers saw text flash by without reading it.

## How to detect it

- A shot with on-screen text shorter than its word count / 3 words per second + 0.5 s.
- Text that appears and leaves within one beat.

## The fix

Extend text shots to their reading time, or cut the text. Re-balance the rhythm with faster cuts on shots that carry no text.
