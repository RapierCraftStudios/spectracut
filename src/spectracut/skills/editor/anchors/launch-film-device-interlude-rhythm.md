# Taste anchor: Device-led launch rhythm with abstract interludes

The strongest product launch films put the product on screen, on a laptop or a phone, and let it carry the story. Between device shots, short abstract interludes (shape, light and colour drawn from the brand) give the eye a breath and reset the rhythm. Every cut lands on the music's beat; a match cut carries a shape or motion from the interlude into the device, and the device shot eases in with a short speed ramp.

## What to take from it

- Device shots are the spine; interludes are punctuation, a beat or two long.
- Cut on the beat. Let the VO lead or trail a cut (J/L) rather than cut into a line.
- Join device and interlude with a match cut and a 0.5 -> 1.0 ramp on the device head.
- Hold every title for its reading time, then move on.
