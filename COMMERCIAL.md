# Commercial licence

> **Draft.** These terms are pending approval by the SpectraCut maintainers (tracked in issue #10). Nothing here is an offer until a signed agreement exists.

SpectraCut is dual-licensed. The default is [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). A commercial licence is an alternative for organisations that cannot or do not want to meet the AGPL's conditions. The full explanation is in [docs/licensing.md](docs/licensing.md).

## What a commercial licence grants

- Use, modify and host SpectraCut, including as a network service, **without** the AGPL obligation to publish your source or offer it to users.
- Embed or redistribute SpectraCut inside proprietary products.
- A written agreement, with terms, support and indemnity options set per customer.

## Who needs one

- You embed SpectraCut in closed-source software you distribute.
- You operate SpectraCut (modified or not) as a service and do not want to offer your modifications under the AGPL.
- Your policy forbids AGPL software.

If you run SpectraCut unmodified for your own use, or publish your changes under the AGPL, you do not need one.

## What it does not cover

- Third-party tools and models that SpectraCut drives as separate programs; their licences stay with their owners.
- Licences of media you use (voices, music, imagery). SpectraCut records them per asset; a commercial SpectraCut licence does not make non-commercial media commercial-safe.
- SpectraCut names, logos and trademarks, except as the agreement states.
